I recently took on the role of Principal Investigator at the Alan Turing Institute. I am also part of the Science Advisory Team at the Alan Turing Institute.

I finished my PhD at Warwick, having been a doctoral researcher at The Alan Turing Institute, during which I held a visiting PhD position at Imperial College London. Previously, I studied at the University of Manchester. 

My research focuses on AI and emerging technologies in organisation and industrial markets. I’m also interested in developing analytical methods and tools for modelling complex and new forms of economic and market data.

Beyond my daily routine, I play tennis.
