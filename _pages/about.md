I recently finished my PhD at Warwick, having been a doctoral researcher at The Alan Turing Institute, during which I held a visiting PhD position at Imperial College London. I also received my doctoral training at the University of Warwick, following ealier studies at the University of Manchester. I’m currently part of the Science Advisory Team at The Alan Turing Institute.

My research focuses on AI and emerging technologies in organisation and industrial markets. I’m also interested in developing methods and tools for modelling complex and new forms of economic and market data.

Beyond my daily routine, I play tennis.
